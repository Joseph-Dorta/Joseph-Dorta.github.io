What your analysis should accomplish

  1. Interpret: connect vectors, matrices, and results to the decision and the units in the case.
  2. Evaluate and validate: compare alternatives, verify calculations, test assumptions, and distinguish model evidence from real-world evidence.
  3. Communicate: explain a recommendation to a nontechnical leader and provide a reproducible explanation for a technical reader.

Assignment deadlines, submission instructions, and the Army-style memo template will be provided by your instructor.
