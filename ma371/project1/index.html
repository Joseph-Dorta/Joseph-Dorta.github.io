MA371 · Project 1

Four projects.
Five disciplines.

Choose the project connected to your major. Work through its worksheet during class, use the interactive calculations to test your reasoning, and make an evidence-based recommendation in an independently authored Army memo.

How to begin

  1. Download your project's PDF worksheet and record your predictions and explanations there.
  2. Open the project page. Run its calculations in stages, interpreting each result before continuing.
  3. Challenge the model with the provided check or sensitivity test, then save the numerical evidence you will cite in your memo and annex.
