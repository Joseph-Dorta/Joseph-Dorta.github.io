One Link, Many Paths

Use a network model to inform a single-link decision, then test the model's assumptions.

Work collaboratively during class; write the final memo and annex independently. This page follows the printable LaTeX packet. Record your reasoning there or in your own notes. Each link to the laboratory points to one calculation, not an all-at-once answer.

The decision: the director of a fictional predeployment training portal can add exactly one navigation link to a Reference Card (R). Option 1 adds Home (H) → R; Option 2 adds Planning Guide (P) → R. Which edit, if either, should the director make to improve modeled discoverability? What evidence would be needed before claiming that actual cadets will find or use R more often? All pages, links, and click records are synthetic.

Course connections: Lesson 17, eigenvalues and eigenvectors; Lesson 18, diagonalization; Lesson 19, applications. The directed adjacency and link-following matrices are introduced here. You need to interpret the eigenvalue-1 stationary vector, not compute a fifth-degree characteristic polynomial.

1. Learn a directed-link rule

An arrow j → i means page j contains a link to page i; it does not imply a reverse link. For a three-page example, let A link to B and C, B to C, and C to A and B. The columns of the link-following matrix are origins and its rows are destinations:

           A     B     C
   A       0     0    1/2
   B      1/2    0    1/2
   C      1/2    1     0

A link is selected uniformly among the links on the current page. Each origin column therefore sums to 1.

  1. Why is this matrix not symmetric? Explain its B,A and C,B entries in words.
  2. Starting certainly on A, use the column vector p⃗0 = (1,0,0)T. Compute Tp⃗0 and interpret every coordinate.

Build the three-page graph and verify the arrows before revealing T.

Dangling-page convention: if a page has no outgoing links, replace its zero T column by the uniform column u⃗ = (1/5)1⃗. This keeps probability mass from disappearing; it is not a claim that a visitor clicked a nonexistent link.

Construct the graph and A, then normalize T with Python after making a column prediction.

3. Explain the PageRank update

At each navigation step, the modeled visitor follows a link with probability α = 0.85 or restarts uniformly with probability 1 − α. Thus G = αT + (1 − α)u⃗1⃗T, and the 5 × 1 probability vector changes by p⃗k+1 = Gp⃗k. A coordinate gives the modeled share on that page after k steps.

This is a discrete dynamical system: updates occur in separate steps, the distribution changes, and the rule is linear matrix multiplication. Repeating the fixed rule gives p⃗k = Gkp⃗0. The links and click rule are assumed constant during the calculation.

Worked three-page bridge: starting on A with α = 0.85, the follow-link contribution is (0, .425, .425)T and uniform restart adds (.05, .05, .05)T. Therefore Gex(1,0,0)T = (.05, .475, .475)T. This is one step, not the long-run ranking.

  1. Verify the C column of Gex from the three arrows. Why are all its entries positive and each column sum 1?
  2. For the five-page baseline, start with p⃗0 = u⃗. Calculate the R coordinate of p⃗1 by hand; separate the link and restart terms.
  3. A stationary vector p⃗* satisfies Gp⃗* = p⃗*. Which eigenvalue does this represent? Why normalize its nonnegative entries to sum to 1?
  4. What does a small numerical residual ‖Gp⃗* − p⃗*‖1 establish, and what does it not establish about real people?

With uniform restarts and 0 < α < 1, G is positive, and this model has a unique positive stationary distribution reached from any starting distribution. If G has an eigenvector basis, diagonalization gives Gk = PDkP−1; do not assume every directed-link matrix is diagonalizable.

Build G, advance one distribution, then find and check the stationary vector.

Optional ASDS extension: an evidence-limited hybrid model

Construct a hybrid T by using observed click proportions for H, P, and E, but retaining the structural equal-link columns for S and R because their click records are absent. For a proposed link, assign a hypothetical click share q to it and scale the origin's observed old-link proportions by 1 − q. This lets you explore a decision boundary without claiming to have measured the proposed links.

  1. Identify precisely which hybrid columns are empirical and which are structural. How does missingness affect the credibility of the overall ranking?
  2. Try q₁ = q₂ = .10, then find a pair of plausible hypothetical shares that reverses which option ranks higher for R. Record both inputs and outputs.
  3. What would an A/B comparison measure after deployment? How would you distinguish a changed click rate from increased comprehension or actual card use?

Run the hybrid sensitivity extension after completing the core comparison. It is an assessment of assumptions, not a replacement for the core model.

AI as a classroom coach

During the designated class session, an approved AI tool may explain a definition, ask about an assumption, or critique a calculation. Verify its output against the link list and arithmetic. Record the tool, purpose, assistance, and verification. Do not provide real portal logs, personnel information, or sensitive data.

7. Write an independently authored Army memo

Before leaving class, record your own claim and criterion, model evidence, limitation or counterevidence, and next evidence needed. A conditional recommendation or deferral is appropriate if the behavioral assumptions are weak.

  • Memo: at most two pages. Use the Army-style template addressed to the portal director. Recommend H → R, P → R, or deferral, and explain the numerical comparison in language a nontechnical leader can use.
  • Separate technical annex: at most three pages. Document link-to-matrix construction, a one-step calculation, stationary-eigenvector interpretation, both options, held-out critique, and sensitivity. An export supports reproducibility but is not a substitute for explanation.

The memo and annex must be your own writing, without AI drafting or rewriting. Typing and the supplied numerical calculator are allowed. If you used AI in class, disclose its limited role and your verification. Due date and submission method will be supplied by your instructor.
