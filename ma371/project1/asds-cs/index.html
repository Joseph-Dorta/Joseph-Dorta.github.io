One Link, Many Paths

Which single navigation link should a training portal add to make its Reference Card easier to find?

This shared track turns directed links into a PageRank model, then asks whether held-out click behavior supports its assumptions. Option 1 adds Home → Reference Card; Option 2 adds Planning Guide → Reference Card. Exactly one link may be added. All pages and click records are synthetic.

Open PageRank laboratoryRead guided inquiry

Model boundary: a PageRank share is a result of an assumed navigation rule. It is not a measured share of cadets, a content-quality score, or evidence that anyone learned from a page. Do not enter real portal logs or sensitive information.
