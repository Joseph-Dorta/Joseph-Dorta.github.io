Project 1 teaching design

Four disciplinary inquiries give cadets a decision to inform with linear algebra. Each project begins with a worksheet, develops a model, uses staged calculations, tests a limitation, and ends in an independently authored Army memo with a technical annex.

The learning sequence

  1. Frame a decision. Cadets identify the audience, action, criterion, and quantities that can inform it.
  2. Construct a model. The worksheet introduces the needed objects and asks cadets to build or interpret them before using the calculator.
  3. Predict, calculate, interpret. Small browser-Python steps return numerical and visual results. Cadets explain each result in the problem's units and context. Code is available for inspection but editing it is optional.
  4. Challenge the result. A held-out check, exact audit, or sensitivity scenario exposes what the model establishes and where assumptions matter.
  5. Communicate the decision. A concise memo addresses a nontechnical leader. A separate annex makes the mathematical reasoning and numerical evidence auditable by a technical reader.

What the work makes visible

The worksheet captures cadets' representations, predictions, interpretations, and revisions. The calculators preserve exact inputs in exported evidence, so numerical rounding in a display does not feed into later steps. The memo asks for a recommendation that follows from the modeled evidence; the annex asks for the mathematical formulation, a check, and a limitation. Together these products address interpreting, evaluating, and validating results in context, and communicating technical information to technical and nontechnical audiences.

The cases and data are synthetic teaching materials. The site provides a way to investigate the models; it does not claim measured improvements in learning or validated operational predictions.

AI boundary

Approved AI may coach cadets during the designated classroom inquiry when its assistance is documented and checked. The final memo and annex must be independently authored without generative AI. The supplied numerical calculators remain permitted. Each project page gives its full instructions.
